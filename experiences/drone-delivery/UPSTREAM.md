fixed-step stabilized flight model, eight delivery routes, package matching,

keyboard, gamepad, and touch controls. Mint-generated runtime models and audio
load from verified immutable Mint CDN artifacts recorded in `mint-assets.json`;
generated local copies, tests, repository metadata, deployment tooling, and
source screenshots were excluded from the capsule.

Eight routes of pastel rooftops. One battery per run. Load up at the dispatch pad and set each package down on its matching ring — before wind, wires, or an empty battery bring you down.

How to play

EnterWS↑↓←→ plays in your browser · gamepad & touch too

How to play

1 Arm & lift off

Press Enter to spin up the rotors, then hold W to climb. Centre the sticks and the drone settles into a hover.

2 Load a package

Your first parcel loads automatically — you start parked on the teal dispatch pad. For the next ones, land back on the pad and the package clips on underneath. Its color tells you which rooftop wants it.

3 Deliver it

Follow the dashed guide line and land inside the matching rooftop ring. Softer touchdowns closer to the centre earn more stars.

Watch out for

Wind drifts you off line. Touching anything — buildings, power lines, trees, the crane jib, the blimp — ends the run. Hard landings bruise the package, and the battery only ever counts down.

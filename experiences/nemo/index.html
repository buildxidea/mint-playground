The Water

Three things out there.
One of them is on your side.

Every model in the game — and in the scene above — was generated in Mint and dropped straight into three.js.

The Racer

That is you

Cruises at 14. Holds Shift for 23 until the reserve runs dry. Nothing you do on your own gets you past that.

Bubble Rings

The points

Thread the middle, not the edge. +100 a ring, multiplied by a combo that climbs to ×8 and dies the moment you miss one.

The Jellyfish

Both at once

Land on the bell and launch to 36 — faster than you can ever swim. Catch the tentacles hanging under it and drop to 7.

Controls

Five keys. One risky habit.

The fish always swims forward. All you decide is where it points and how hard it pushes.

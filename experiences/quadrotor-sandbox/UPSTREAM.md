# Upstream provenance

Quadrotor Sandbox was imported from
[`CloudyLo001/quadrotorsim`](https://github.com/CloudyLo001/quadrotorsim) at
commit `a6f968cfb91dc63992f0802968bdb8564e95f032`.

The upstream repository did not declare a repository-wide software license.
On 2026-08-05, the developer explicitly approved all publication gates for
adding the project to Play with `status: "published"`, including publication
of the Mint Playground adaptation in `mintdotgg/mint-playground` under that
mirror's MIT license. This permission applies to the Playground adaptation
only and does not claim or change a license for the upstream repository. The
adaptation preserves the upstream Vite and Three.js architecture, seven-craft
roster, three flight models, Rapier collision world, camera and sensing modes,
instrumentation, wind, landing grades, and deterministic flight-core tests.

Mint-generated aircraft, city buildings, props, audio, and panorama load from
their verified immutable Mint CDN artifacts and are recorded in
`mint-assets.json`. The 32 local generated runtime files, preview images,
Windows-only asset-sync command, npm lockfile, Git metadata, and build outputs
were excluded from the portable capsule.

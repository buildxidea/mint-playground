# Upstream provenance

Moodboard to Room was imported from the `moodboard/` application in
[`sofi-le/mint-1`](https://github.com/sofi-le/mint-1) at commit
`5da4c309cbee3652691e36363a225a2beca468da`.

The upstream repository did not declare a repository-wide software license.
On 2026-08-17, the developer explicitly approved all publication gates for
adding the project to Play with `status: "published"`, including publication
of the Mint Playground adaptation in `mintdotgg/mint-playground` under that
mirror's MIT license. This permission applies to the Playground adaptation
only and does not claim or change a license for the upstream repository.

The adaptation preserves the upstream Vite and vanilla Three.js architecture;
eight guest briefs; one-question interviews; limited-kit selection; furniture,
poster, paint, and lighting controls; guest interruptions; deterministic hidden
quirks; judging; retries; and guestbook progression. The legacy generation
ledger and previews page, npm and pnpm lockfiles, Git metadata, build output,
and local dependencies were excluded.

The thirteen furniture models and previews, three warm-oak material maps, and
six poster images load from verified immutable Mint CDN artifacts recorded in
`mint-assets.json`. The 48 local generated runtime files (38,168,489 bytes)
were excluded from the portable capsule.
